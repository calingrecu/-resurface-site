# Passing Thoughts and Siri

Every phrase includes the app's name, *Passing Thoughts*. Say **"thought"**, not "note":
Siri sends "add a note" to Apple's Notes app, even with our name in the sentence.

## Save a thought

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| *"Add a thought to Passing Thoughts"* | Siri asks *"What's the thought?"*, saves what you say as a note and replies *"Saved to Passing Thoughts."* The app doesn't open. Works on a locked iPhone. |
| *"Write a thought in Passing Thoughts"*, *"Save a thought in Passing Thoughts"* | The same. |
| *"Record a thought in Passing Thoughts"*, *"New thought in Passing Thoughts"* | Opens the recorder, as tapping Speak does. Needs the iPhone unlocked. |

Saving a thought never needs the switch below: Siri gives the app your words, and the app
saves them on your iPhone.

## Ask about your notes

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| *"What's on this week in Passing Thoughts?"* | The notes dated this week. Also: today, tomorrow, this weekend, next week, next weekend, this month, next month. |
| *"What needs review in Passing Thoughts?"* | Your open tasks and pending decisions. |
| *"Ask Passing Thoughts"* | Siri asks *"What do you want to ask?"*; the answer is your notes that answer it. |
| *"Search Passing Thoughts"* | Opens Search. |
| *"Open Parked in Passing Thoughts"* | Opens a screen: Home, Ask, Search or Parked. |

## "Siri can read my notes" (Settings → Siri)

**Off** (the default): the app gives Siri none of your notes. A question opens the app on
the answer instead, with a line saying why.

**On**: Siri answers by itself, without opening the app: *"1 note. Home visit scheduled: The
rescue confirmed the home visit for November 14…"*. Your notes are also added to your
iPhone's Spotlight search, and the new Siri can find a note from a description. To answer,
Siri may use Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which Apple says does not store your data.
Turning the switch off removes your notes from Siri and Spotlight.

One thing no app can control: when you invoke the new Siri, iOS may let it read what is on
your screen, including an open note. To prevent that, turn off Siri in iPhone Settings, or
choose Siri Classic in Screen Time.

## Shortcuts and the Action Button

The same actions are in the Shortcuts app, so you can put *Record a Thought* on the Action
Button. Shortcuts also offers *Find Notes* and *Get Note Text* for shortcuts you build;
those need the switch on to see your notes.

Questions, opening a screen and recording need the iPhone unlocked.
