# Passing Thoughts — Support

Passing Thoughts is a private notes app: speak, type or scan a thought, and it is saved,
titled, and found again when you ask, with its AI running on your iPhone. New here? Read the
[user guide](/guide/) and the [Siri phrases](/siri/).

**Contact: info@passingthoughtsapp.com** — include the app version from
Settings (the line at the bottom) if you can.

## Requirements

- iOS 26 or later.
- AI features (titles, review of open items, Ask) require Apple Intelligence. Capture,
  transcription, scanning, and search work without it.
- Live Scan needs an iPhone that supports the live text scanner; on others the tile is
  hidden and Photo Scan still works.

## Frequently asked

**Is my data private?**
Yes. The app keeps your notes on your iPhone and sends nothing anywhere: no account, no
server, no analytics. The one exception is yours to make: with Settings → Siri → "Siri can
read my notes" on, Siri can read your notes to answer you, and may use Apple's Private Cloud
Compute to do it. It is off by default. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) and
Settings → About Privacy.

**Siri sent my thought to Apple Notes.**
Say "thought", not "note": *"Add a thought to Passing Thoughts."* Siri gives "add a note" to
Apple Notes even with our name in the sentence. All phrases: [Siri](/siri/).

**Siri opened the app instead of answering.**
"Siri can read my notes" is off, so the app shows the answer itself. Turn it on in
Settings → Siri if you want Siri to answer.

**Does scanning keep my photos?**
No. Live Scan reads one camera frame in memory and drops it. Photo Scan reads the photo you
pick and leaves it where it is; the app keeps no copy.

**Why do AI features seem off?**
Check Settings → Processing → **AI status**. It tells you honestly whether the
on-device model is ready, still downloading, or needs Apple Intelligence
turned on in iOS Settings.

**The transcript looks different from what I said.**
Passing Thoughts shows a version cleaned for readability. Tap **"view original"** on
the note to see the exact raw transcript. Editing the note always starts from
the text you see.

**A note says "Too long for AI features" or shows a language badge.**
Very long notes and notes not in English are saved and searchable as normal,
but the AI features skip them — the badge is the app telling you that
honestly rather than guessing.

**Ask showed nothing, but I think the answer is in my notes.**
Ask shows only your own notes, and only the ones that answer the question; when
none does, it says so instead of guessing. Try rephrasing with the words your
note actually uses.

**Where did insight cards go?**
Version 1.2 removed them. On the newest iOS they too often put unrelated notes
together, so the app no longer shows them. Your notes are untouched.

**Where do reminders go?**
Into a "Passing Thoughts" list in your own Apple Reminders app, only when you ask for
one.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete a note from its ••• menu, or use Settings → "Delete All Local Data" to remove
everything at once. Deleting the app removes everything it stored too. The app keeps nothing
anywhere else; with the Siri switch on, deleted notes also leave Spotlight.
