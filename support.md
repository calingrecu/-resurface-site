# Passing Thoughts — Support

Passing Thoughts is a private voice-notes app: speak a thought, and it is saved,
transcribed, titled, and found again when you ask — entirely on your iPhone.

**Contact: info@passingthoughtsapp.com** — include the app version from
Settings (the line at the bottom) if you can.

## Requirements

- iOS 26 or later.
- AI features (titles, review of open items, Ask) require Apple Intelligence. Capture,
  transcription, and search work without it.

## Frequently asked

**Is my data private?**
Yes — everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no cloud, no analytics.
See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/). You can verify the app's privacy
statement in Settings → About.

**Why do AI features seem off?**
Check Settings → Processing → **AI status**. It tells you honestly whether the
on-device model is ready, still downloading, or needs Apple Intelligence
turned on in iOS Settings.

**The transcript looks different from what I said.**
Passing Thoughts shows a version cleaned for readability. Tap **"view original"** on
the note to see the exact raw transcript. Editing the note always starts from
the text you see.

**A note says "Too long for AI features" or shows a language badge.**
Very long notes and notes not in English are saved and searchable as normal,
but the AI features skip them — the badge is the app telling you that
honestly rather than guessing.

**Ask showed nothing, but I think the answer is in my notes.**
Ask shows only your own notes, and only the ones that answer the question; when
none does, it says so instead of guessing. Try rephrasing with the words your
note actually uses.

**Where did insight cards go?**
Version 1.2 removed them. On the newest iOS they too often put unrelated notes
together, so the app no longer shows them. Your notes are untouched.

**Where do reminders go?**
Into a "Passing Thoughts" list in your own Apple Reminders app, only when you ask for
one.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete notes individually in the app, or delete the app itself — everything it
stored goes with it. Nothing exists anywhere else.
