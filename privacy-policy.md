# Resurface — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-07-20**

Resurface is a voice-notes app made by Calin Grecu. This policy is short
because the honest answer is short: **Resurface does not collect, transmit, or
share any data. Everything stays on your iPhone.**

## What we collect

Nothing. Resurface has no server, no account system, no analytics, no ads, no
tracking, and no third-party software components. The app makes no network
connections. Nothing you record, type, or do in the app is ever sent to us or
to anyone else.

## Where your data lives

- **Your notes** (transcripts, titles, tags, and the app's AI-derived
  organization) are stored only on your device.
- **Voice recordings** are processed on your device and deleted once the
  transcript is safely saved. Audio is never transmitted anywhere.
- **Transcription** uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your audio does
  not leave the device for transcription.
- **AI features** (titles, insights, questions and answers) run on Apple
  Intelligence, on-device. Your notes are never sent to a cloud AI service.
- Like most iOS apps, Resurface's data is included in your device backups
  (iCloud or computer) according to your iOS backup settings. That is Apple's
  system backup under your control — not us transmitting anything.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Microphone** — to record your voice notes.
- **Speech recognition** — to transcribe them, on-device.
- **Reminders** (optional) — only when you ask Resurface to create a reminder;
  it is written to your own Reminders app.
- **Notifications** (optional) — for reminders you opt into. Notification
  content is composed on-device.

Denying any permission simply disables that feature; nothing else changes.

## Diagnostics

Resurface can produce a diagnostic export to help troubleshoot a problem. It
is created only when you ask for it, and it goes only where you choose to send
it via the iOS share sheet. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

## Deleting your data

Delete any note in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored
on the device. There is nothing to delete anywhere else, because nothing ever
left your iPhone.

## Children

Resurface is not directed at children. Since the app collects no data from
anyone, it collects no data from children either.

## Changes to this policy

If Resurface ever changes what it does with data (for example, an optional
feature that sends something at your request), this policy will be updated
here before that version ships, and the app's own privacy description will say
the same thing.
