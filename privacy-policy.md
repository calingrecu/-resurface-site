# Passing Thoughts — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-11-08** (the previous version was dated 2026-07-26)

Passing Thoughts is a notes app for iPhone: you speak, type or scan a thought, and it is
saved, organized and found again. This policy is short because the honest answer is short:
**Passing Thoughts does not collect any data, and the app itself sends nothing anywhere.**
The one exception is yours to make: if you turn on "Siri can read my notes", Apple's Siri can
read your notes to answer you, as described below.

## What we collect

Nothing. Passing Thoughts has no server, no account system, no analytics, no ads, no
tracking, and no third-party software components. The app makes no network
connections. Nothing you record, type, scan, or do in the app is ever sent to us or
to anyone else.

## Where your data lives

- **Your notes** (transcripts, typed and scanned text, titles, tags, and the app's
  AI-derived organization) are stored only on your device.
- **Voice recordings** are processed on your device and, by default, deleted
  once the transcript is safely saved — unless you turn on "Keep recordings
  after transcription" in Settings, in which case recordings stay on your
  device until you delete them, delete their notes, or turn the setting back
  off (which offers to delete the retained recordings right then). Audio is
  never transmitted anywhere.
- **Transcription** uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your audio does
  not leave the device for transcription.
- **Scanning text** happens on your device. Live Scan reads one camera frame in memory
  and discards it once the text is read; no photo is saved, to the app or to your Photos
  library. Photo Scan reads the text in a photo you pick; the photo stays where it is, and
  the app keeps no copy of it.
- **AI features** (titles, review of open items, and Ask) run on Apple
  Intelligence, on-device. The app never sends your notes to a cloud AI service.
- Like most iOS apps, Passing Thoughts's data is included in your device backups
  (iCloud or computer) according to your iOS backup settings. That is Apple's
  system backup under your control — not us transmitting anything.

## Siri and Spotlight

Passing Thoughts works with Siri, Shortcuts and the Action Button.

- **Saving a thought through Siri** ("Add a thought to Passing Thoughts") saves what you
  say as a note on your iPhone. Siri hears your words because you are speaking to Siri;
  that is Apple's service, covered by Apple's privacy policy.
- **"Siri can read my notes"** (Settings → Siri) is **off by default**. While it is off,
  the app gives Siri no note content. If you turn it on, Siri and Apple Intelligence can
  find your notes and read them to answer you, and your notes are added to your iPhone's
  on-device Spotlight index. To answer, Siri may use Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which
  Apple says does not store your data. Turning the switch off removes your notes from Siri
  and Spotlight.
- **What is on your screen:** when you invoke the new Siri, iOS may let Siri see what is on
  your screen, including an open note, whether or not the switch is on. That is a system
  feature no app can turn off. To prevent it, turn off Siri in iPhone Settings, or choose
  Siri Classic in Screen Time.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Microphone** — to record your voice notes.
- **Speech recognition** — to transcribe them, on-device.
- **Camera** (optional) — only while you use Live Scan to read text into a note. No photo
  is saved. Photo Scan uses the system photo picker and asks for no Photos permission: the
  app sees only the photo you pick.
- **Reminders** (optional) — only when you ask Passing Thoughts to create a reminder;
  it is written to your own Reminders app.
- **Notifications** (optional) — for reminders you opt into. Notification
  content is composed on-device.

Denying any permission simply disables that feature; nothing else changes.

## Diagnostics

Passing Thoughts can produce a diagnostic export to help troubleshoot a problem. It
is created only when you ask for it, and it goes only where you choose to send
it via the iOS share sheet. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

## Deleting your data

Delete any note in the app, or use Settings → "Delete All Local Data" to
remove everything at once — notes, recordings, AI-derived data, logs, and
pending or delivered reminder notifications — in a single step. A deleted note also
leaves Spotlight. You can also delete the app to remove everything it stored on the
device. The app keeps nothing anywhere else.

## Children

Passing Thoughts is not directed at children. Since the app collects no data from
anyone, it collects no data from children either.

## Changes to this policy

If Passing Thoughts ever changes what it does with data (for example, an optional
feature that sends something at your request), this policy will be updated
here before that version ships, and the app's own privacy description will say
the same thing.

Version 1.3 added the optional Siri switch above without updating this page; this version
corrects that.
