# Passing Thoughts

**Your second brain on iPhone. Speak, type or scan a thought; ask for it back later.**

Passing Thoughts catches a thought before it disappears, keeps it on your iPhone, and brings
it back when you need it. Its AI runs on the iPhone itself. No account, no servers, no
tracking.

<a href="https://apps.apple.com/app/id6793355936"><img src="/assets/app-store-badge.svg" alt="Download on the App Store" height="40"></a>

## Four ways to catch a thought

- **Speak.** Tap Speak and talk. Your words appear on screen as you say them, and the
  recording is saved the moment you stop. The transcript is cleaned up for reading, with the
  original one tap away.
- **Type.** A blank note, nothing else in the way.
- **Scan.** *Live Scan* reads text through the camera: a receipt, a label, a page of a book.
  *Photo Scan* reads the text in a photo you already have. No photo is saved.
- **Tell Siri.** Say *"Add a thought to Passing Thoughts"*, and Siri asks what the thought
  is and saves it, even on a locked iPhone. [More about Siri](/siri/)

Every note gets a title, so you can find it at a glance.

## Ask, and get your own words back

Type a question like *"How much is the adoption fee?"* and Passing Thoughts shows the notes
that answer it, with the answering sentence in bold. It never writes an answer of its own,
so it has nothing to make up. When no note answers, it says so.

Ask about time too: *"What's on this week?"*, *"What's on next month?"*. A note that says
"next Tuesday" is read from the day you wrote it.

Or ask Siri: *"What's on this week in Passing Thoughts?"*

## Nothing slips through

Open tasks and pending decisions come back to you in **Needs Review**, each with a short
reason: *"You still need to fix the gap in the backyard fence before the home visit on
November 14."* Mark it done, keep it open, or set a reminder. Notes whose moment has passed
are set aside in **Parked**, never deleted.

## Private by design

- Transcription, scanning, search and all of the app's AI run on your iPhone.
- No account, no server, no analytics, no tracking. The app makes no network connections.
- Siri reads your notes only if you turn on *"Siri can read my notes"*.
