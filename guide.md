# Passing Thoughts — User guide

## 1. Make a note

Home opens on **Choose how to create a new note**.

**Speak.** Tap the wide **Speak** button and talk. Your words appear on screen as you speak.
Tap stop and the note is saved at once; the transcript and a title fill in a moment later. A
phone call or the time limit stops the recording and **saves** it; nothing is lost. If the
room is quiet the app tells you, but it never stops on its own.

**Type.** **Type** opens a blank note. Write and save; the first line becomes the title until
the AI writes a better one. What you type is kept exactly as you wrote it.

**Live Scan.** Point the camera at text (a receipt, a whiteboard, a page). The text it sees
is highlighted. Tap a highlighted block to take just that, or **Capture Text** for everything
in view. Correct the text if needed, then save. The camera frame is read in memory and
dropped; no photo is saved.

**Photo Scan.** Pick a photo and the app reads its text into a new note to correct and save.
The photo stays where it was; the app keeps no copy and asks for no Photos permission.

**Siri.** Say *"Add a thought to Passing Thoughts"*. Siri asks *"What's the thought?"* and
saves your answer, even on a locked iPhone. See [Siri](/siri/) for every phrase.

> **Example.** On the way out of the vet's: *"Add a thought to Passing Thoughts."* — *"The
> adoption fee is 250 and covers her shots; they hand over the vet records at pickup."*

## 2. What happens to a note

- **A title.** Every note gets one, and you can edit it.
- **A clean transcript.** Spoken notes are tidied for reading ("um", repeated words). Tap
  **view original** to see exactly what was heard. Typed and scanned notes are never changed.
- **Tags.** Short tags make a note easier to find; add or edit your own.
- **All on your iPhone.** Transcription, scanning and the AI run on the iPhone.

## 3. Find it again

Open **Recall** at the bottom of the screen.

**Ask** answers questions with your own notes. Type a question and press Return:

- *"How much is the adoption fee?"* shows the note, with the answering sentence in bold.
- *"When can I pick up my new lenses?"* shows both the order and the pickup call, each with
  its date, so you see which came last.
- *"What's on next week?"*, *"What's on this weekend?"*, *"What's on next month?"* show the
  notes dated in that period. "Next Tuesday" in a note is read from the day you wrote it.

Ask never writes an answer of its own; when no note answers, it says so. Your last five
questions are listed under the box; tap one to ask it again.

**Search** finds notes by their words, as you type. Turn on **Include Inactive Notes** to
search parked notes too.

## 4. Needs Review

When a note holds an open task or a decision you haven't made, Home shows **Needs Review**
with a count. Each item gives a short reason, for example:

> **Cousin's graduation** — pending decision
> You need to decide whether to drive up the night before or very early the next morning for
> Priya's graduation on November 29.

- **Done**: the item goes away (it comes back only if you change the note).
- **Keep open**: it stays.
- **Remind**: adds a reminder to your own Apple Reminders, with the date from the note
  filled in.

## 5. Parked

Notes whose moment has passed (an appointment last month, a finished errand) move to
**Recall → Parked**, with the reason. Nothing is deleted. Tap **Unpark** to bring one back,
or park a note yourself from its ••• menu.

## 6. Settings worth knowing

- **Processing → AI status** says whether Apple Intelligence is ready on this iPhone.
- **Processing → AI features** turns every AI feature off at once. Capture, scanning and
  search keep working.
- **Keep recordings after transcription** keeps the audio of spoken notes; off by default.
- **Siri → Siri can read my notes** lets Siri answer from your notes; off by default. See
  [Siri](/siri/).
- **Delete All Local Data** removes everything in one step. To delete one note, use its •••
  menu.

## 7. Limits, honestly

- AI features need Apple Intelligence and work on English notes. A note in another language,
  or a very long one, is saved and searchable, and shows a small label saying the AI skipped
  it.
- The AI can miss an open task, or flag one that isn't. **Keep open** and **Done** are there
  for that.
